About the pilot

From an observation to a map

Analem Jacob started this project after exploring Project Squirrel in the Citizen Sciences in Landscape Ecology lab at UBC Forestry (MGEM). This trial explores how community observations could help identify areas for further review.

How the trial works

Open the site once online and wait for the offline indicator. Then use a fictional location and test what happens when you save an observation with no signal. Saved examples stay in your outbox. You can export them or sync them in the local database trial.
