Context
Natural Earth Vector v5.1.2: 1:50m administrative boundaries of neighboring
countries, 1:10m rivers and lake centerlines, lakes, and populated places.
Selected, clipped, or reduced to cartographic attributes for this pilot.
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson
Public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Reference labels are placed for readability. Map boundaries and hydrography
are generalized and intended for orientation.

Southern river references
© OpenStreetMap contributors via the Geofabrik Ghana free shapefile extract.
Includes named mapped segments of the Ankobra, Birim, Offin (Ofin), Pra and
Tano rivers. Selected river names, simplified geometry, and retained OSM IDs.
The layer shows reference segments and does not represent watershed coverage.
https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/ghana.html
